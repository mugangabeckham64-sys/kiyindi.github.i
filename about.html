Our Background

Who We Are

Kiyndi Foundation School is a leading institution committed to excellence in education. Established with a strong foundation of discipline and innovation, the school aims to provide students with both academic knowledge and life skills that prepare them for the future.

Over the years, we have grown into a center of excellence, producing well-rounded students who excel in academics, sports, and leadership. We are dedicated to nurturing future leaders through quality teaching and a supportive, safe environment.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Aims

The core principles that guide everything we do at Kiyndi Foundation School.

V Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in education, producing responsible, innovative, and globally competitive graduates who are proud to serve their communities.

M Our Mission

To provide high-quality education that develops academic excellence, moral values, and practical skills in a safe, supportive, and inspiring environment.

A Our Aims

To equip every student with critical thinking, leadership qualities, and the academic foundation needed to succeed at higher levels of education and in life.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These values are woven into every aspect of school life — from the classroom to the playground.

Academic Excellence

We hold high expectations for every student and provide the resources and support to help them meet those standards.

Integrity & Discipline

Honesty and self-discipline are foundational to our school culture and guide how we treat each other every day.

Teamwork & Respect

We celebrate diversity and teach students to collaborate with empathy, kindness, and respect for all people.

Innovation & Creativity

We encourage curiosity, original thinking, and creative problem-solving as essential 21st-century skills.

Community & Service

We instill a sense of social responsibility and pride in contributing positively to Uganda and the wider world.

Holistic Development

Education goes beyond books — we nurture the physical, emotional, social, and spiritual growth of every child.
