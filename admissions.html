Admissions at
Kiyndi Foundation

We welcome students from all backgrounds who are ready to learn, grow, and become responsible citizens. Admissions are open throughout the year.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Recent academic transcripts
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Parent/Guardian ID copy
  • Medical/immunization record
Need help? Contact our admissions office via WhatsApp at +256 700 123 456 or email us at info@kiyndifoundationschool.com
